# Project Bluebird

AI for UK air traffic control. Project Bluebird is an EPSRC Prosperity Partnership between [NATS](https://www.nats.aero/), [The Alan Turing Institute](https://www.turing.ac.uk/) and the [University of Exeter](https://www.exeter.ac.uk/), running since 2021. It is building a probabilistic digital twin of UK en route airspace, AI agents that can control air traffic within it, and the assurance research needed to trust them.

- **Website:** [www.projectbluebird.ai](https://www.projectbluebird.ai/), with [the team](https://www.projectbluebird.ai/about-us/teams/) and [the publications](https://www.projectbluebird.ai/impact/publications/)
- **BluebirdATC:** the open-source digital twin and agent training environment, in [this repository](https://github.com/project-bluebird/BluebirdATC) with [documentation](https://docs.projectbluebird.ai/)
